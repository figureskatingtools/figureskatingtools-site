Privacy notice

Last updated 22 October 2026

This notice explains how personal data is processed in the figureskatingtools.com service. The service is a set of tools for figure skating result services and competition organisers: tools that produce a competition's documents from FS Manager data, tools that run entirely in your browser, tools that process data in memory without storing it, and a live link that receives competition data from FS Manager. The tools are used by signed-in result service operators and organisers' staff, and access is by invitation.

figureskatingtools.com does not host or publish results, and no competition data is public here. The only public pages are the front page, this notice and the terms of use. What the tools produce is downloaded by the operator. Results are published on other sites, and each of those sites is responsible for what it publishes under its own privacy notice.

This notice is available in English and Finnish; the English text prevails.

1Who is responsible

Responsibility is split by whose data it is:

  • Competition data (section 2) is the responsibility of the organisation on whose behalf the service is used, such as the organising club or the result service, or the user personally if they use it for no organisation. The terms of use call it the Customer. The Customer is the controller: it decides that the competition is run, what is exported from FS Manager, who receives judges' papers and what is published. We process competition data on the Customer's behalf, as its processor, under the data processing terms. If the Customer is a result service working for an organiser, the organiser is the controller, the result service its processor and we are a sub-processor.
  • Data about the users and the running of the service (section 3) is our own responsibility. The controller is Markus Lintuala, a private individual in Finland who maintains figureskatingtools.com as a volunteer project. Contact: markus@lintuala.fi.

The service's administrators (currently Markus Lintuala) can view and manage every competition, including a deleted one until its data has been removed: they can correct its details, change its deletion date, hand it over to another user as its owner, restore a competition deleted by mistake or remove its data at once. They do so only to support the Customer, for example when an operator asks for help, or to keep the service running and secure.

2Competition data we process on the Customer's behalf

Competition data is processed to produce a competition's documents, such as judges' papers, the protocol and readable score sheets, and, when needed, redacted versions of a result service's own published result files.

The Customer decides the legal basis. Typically it is the organiser's legitimate interest for competition data and consent for photos. The Customer obtains and keeps the consents; we never see them.

Podium and team photos

A photo may be used only if the person in it has a valid photography consent. For a minor, consent is given by a parent or guardian. Location data and other metadata are removed from the photos in every PDF the tools produce. HEIC photos (the iPhone format) are converted to a clean JPEG as soon as they are uploaded, and so are the photos of a team photo ZIP file. Other uploaded photos are stored as they were uploaded, metadata included, until the competition's data is deleted.

FS Manager participant files

Participant files from FS Manager use the ODF data format. Dates of birth are removed from a participant file before it is stored, whether FS Manager sends it or someone uploads it, so they are not stored. The rest of the file is kept as FS Manager produced it, because the tools read it. That includes the participants' names, club, gender, nationality, the events they are registered for, their team and FS Manager's own participant codes. Gender is kept because competition rules depend on it, for example in categories and in pair and ice dance couples. Nationality is kept because result documents show it, as is customary in the sport. The tools use names, clubs, teams and events and do not print the rest.

Tools that store nothing

Some tools process data in memory only, for as long as a request runs, and store nothing. The GDPR Removal Tool is one: a result service can use it to redact its own published result files, for example when a skater has asked the publishing site to remove their name. It fetches the already public pages and PDF files from the result sites it is set up for, replaces the person's name with the text “Nimi poistettu pyynnöstä” (name removed on request) and blanks their club, keeps the placements and scores, and returns the changed files to the operator as a ZIP file. Working copies exist on the server only while the request runs. Publishing the changed files is up to the result service.

Other tools run entirely in your browser. The Competition Banner Generator is one: the logos you add never leave your device.

Who can see a competition

A competition and everything stored about it is visible only to its owner (normally the user who created it), the users the owner has shared it with, and the administrators. The owner decides whether each of them may only view it or also edit it, and can take the access away again.

The people working on a competition see each other's names and e-mail addresses. The owner sees the names and e-mail addresses of the users the competition is shared with in its access list, and they see the owner's. Everyone with access to a competition also sees the e-mail address of the user who uploaded each of its shared files, of the user who accepted each FS Manager sender, and of the user who first used the competition in each tool.

4Where data is held and who receives it

  • Microsoft Azure hosts the service, its files, databases and technical logs. Everything is stored in the Sweden Central region in the EU. Data sent live from FS Manager reaches the service through Microsoft's Azure Front Door and API Management.
  • Microsoft Entra ID handles sign-in.
  • Mailgun (Mailgun Technologies, Inc., part of Sinch) sends the judges' papers e-mail through its EU region. It receives the recipients' addresses and the message with its download link. Open and click tracking is turned off. Mailgun keeps no copy of the message and keeps delivery event data (recipient address, time and delivery status) for up to 30 days.

Microsoft and Mailgun are US companies, so data may in limited cases be accessed from the United States, for example for support or security. Both companies are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (checked on 13 October 2026). If that framework no longer applies, the EU standard contractual clauses in their data processing terms do.

Data is not sold or shared for marketing. Nothing in the tools is public: everything requires sign-in, except a judges' papers download link sent by e-mail or copied to share, which contains a random code and works for at most 5 days. Anyone who has the link can download the papers, so share it only with the people who need them. Download links shown in the tools expire after 15 minutes.

Sub-processors

For competition data, we use these sub-processors (list dated 22 October 2026):

  • Microsoft: hosting (Microsoft Azure, Sweden Central) and sign-in (Microsoft Entra ID).
  • Mailgun Technologies, Inc.: delivery of the judges' papers e-mail (EU region).

We tell users at least 30 days before we add or replace a sub-processor, by e-mail and in the service. A Customer that objects can stop using the service and delete its competitions before the change takes effect.

Finding other users

When sharing, a signed-in user can find other users who have signed in to the service in two ways: by typing at least 3 characters of their name, or by typing their exact, full e-mail address. Part of an e-mail address finds nobody. A name search shows the name and only a masked e-mail address, for example a•••@club.fi. The full e-mail address is shown only when it was typed in full, or to a user who already has a competition in common with that person (as its owner or through sharing). Searches are limited per user so that the directory cannot be copied.

How data is protected

  • Every tool and every competition requires sign-in, and each competition is visible only to the people described in section 2.
  • Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The tools' back ends can be reached only through the site, which checks who you are and what you may do before passing a request on.
  • An FS Manager computer can deliver data only after an operator has accepted it; until then its files are held apart and are not visible in the competition.
  • Data is deleted automatically (section 5), dates of birth are removed from participant files, and photo metadata is removed from the documents the tools produce.
  • Download links contain a random code and expire.

5How long data is kept

Everything stored about a competition is removed after the competition: the uploaded files and the files sent from FS Manager, rosters, photos, the generated judges' papers and protocols, the e-mail send log, the FS Manager sender records and the list of users it is shared with. Each competition has one deletion date that applies to all of its data in every tool, 60 days after the competition is created. The operator may move the date one week later at a time when there is a reason to, for example when the competition takes place later than that, and the new date applies to every tool alike. An administrator may also set another deletion date, for example when an operator asks for it. The operator may delete the competition earlier, and its data is then removed from every tool the following night.

After deletion, only the per-competition statistics described in section 3 remain: the competition's name, dates and usage counts, without any personal data.

  • Judges' papers download links: at most 5 days, and never longer than the competition's data is kept.
  • Technical logs: at most 90 days.
  • Administrator log: 1 year.
  • FS Manager transfer session records: deleted after 30 days without activity.
  • Mailgun's delivery event data: up to 30 days.
  • Your entry in the user directory: removed after 12 months without signing in, unless you still own a competition whose data has not been deleted. Your access to competitions shared with you is removed with it. You can also ask for it to be removed earlier.

6Cookies and browser storage

The service uses one cookie, the sign-in session cookie (AppServiceAuthSession), which is strictly necessary for signing in. A large session may be split into numbered parts (AppServiceAuthSession1, AppServiceAuthSession2 and so on). The sign-in pages of Microsoft, and of Google if you sign in with Google, set their own cookies under their own privacy statements.

Your browser's local storage keeps, on your own device: the competition you have selected (its name, code, dates, your role and its owner's name and e-mail address), a copy of the “What's New” list for a few minutes, and a display preference of some tools. It stays until you select another competition or clear your browser's site data.

The service does not use cookies or anything else for analytics, marketing or tracking. The fonts are served from this site, so loading a page does not contact any third party.

7Your rights

You have the right to access the data concerning you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data deleted, to restrict its processing, to object to it and to receive the data you have given us in a machine-readable form (data portability, Article 20). If a photo of you has been used on the basis of consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time. A parent or guardian can act on behalf of a minor. Who answers depends on whose data it is:

  • Competition data (section 2): the organiser or result service that ran the competition, because it is the controller. If we receive such a request, we forward it to that organisation without undue delay and help it respond. A photography consent is withdrawn through the club that collected it.
  • Your account and the logs (section 3): us, at markus@lintuala.fi. We reply within one month, or tell you within that time if we need longer and why.

Requests about published results, such as removing your name from a result list, go to the site that publishes those results, because it decides what it publishes and is responsible for it. figureskatingtools.com publishes no results and cannot change other sites.

8Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe your data has been processed in breach of data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi). We hope you will contact us first so that the matter can be put right quickly.
