Privacy notice

Last updated 13 October 2026

← Back to figureskatingtools.com

This notice explains how personal data is processed in the figureskatingtools.com service. The service is a set of tools for figure skating result services: Judge Paper Creator, Protocol Generator, Score Modifier, the Competition Banner Generator, the GDPR Removal Tool and the live link that receives competition data from FS Manager. The tools are used by signed-in result service operators and organisers' staff, and access is by invitation.

The service itself publishes nothing. What the tools produce (judges' papers, protocols, score sheets and banners) is downloaded by the operator. If a document is later published on a results site, such as fs.lintuala.fi, that site's own privacy notice covers the publication.

1Controller and processors

The controller is Markus Lintuala, who maintains figureskatingtools.com. Contact: markus@lintuala.fi.

Signed-in users, such as result service operators and the organising club's staff, process data in the service on the controller's behalf (as processors). They may use the data only for the competition at hand and in line with this notice.

2What data is processed, where it comes from and why

Data is processed to produce a competition's paperwork: judges' papers, the protocol, readable score sheets and data-removal versions of published results, and to keep the service running and secure.

Legitimate interest refers to the EU General Data Protection Regulation, Article 6(1)(f), and consent to Article 6(1)(a).

Podium and team photos

A photo is used only if the person in it has a valid photography consent through their club. For a minor, consent is given by a parent or guardian. Consent can be withdrawn at any time (section 6). Location data and other metadata are removed from the photos in every PDF the tools produce, and HEIC photos (the iPhone format) are converted to a clean JPEG as soon as they are uploaded. Other uploaded photos are stored as they were uploaded, metadata included, until the competition's data is deleted (section 4).

FS Manager participant files

Participant files from FS Manager use the ODF data format, which has room for further details such as date of birth, gender and nationality. Whatever FS Manager fills in there is kept in the file until the competition's data is deleted, but the tools read only names and clubs and never print the rest.

GDPR Removal Tool and Banner Generator

The GDPR Removal Tool fetches the published result pages and PDF files the operator points it to, replaces the person's name and club and hands back a ZIP file. The pages are processed in memory only, and nothing about the request is stored. The Competition Banner Generator runs entirely in your browser: the logos you add never leave your device.

The data is not used for automated decision-making or profiling.

3Where data is held and who receives it

  • Microsoft Azure hosts the service, its files, databases and technical logs. Everything is stored in the Sweden Central region in the EU.
  • Microsoft Entra ID handles sign-in.
  • Mailgun (Mailgun Technologies, Inc., part of Sinch) sends the judges' papers e-mail through its EU region. It receives the recipients' addresses and the message with its download link. Open and click tracking is turned off.

Microsoft and Mailgun are US companies, so data may in limited cases be accessed from the United States, for example for support or security. Both companies are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (checked on 13 October 2026), and their data processing terms also include the EU standard contractual clauses.

Data is not sold or shared for marketing. Nothing in the tools is public: everything requires sign-in, except the judges' papers download link sent by e-mail, which contains a random code and works for at most 5 days.

4How long data is kept

All of a competition's data is deleted automatically 60 days after the competition is created. This covers the uploaded files and the files sent from FS Manager, rosters, photos, the generated judges' papers and protocols, the e-mail send log and the FS Manager sender records. The operator may extend the period one week at a time when there is a reason to, for example when the competition takes place later than that.

If the operator deletes a competition, its data is removed the following night. After deletion, only the competition's name, dates and usage counts remain, as statistics without personal data.

  • Judges' papers download links: at most 5 days.
  • Technical logs (errors and request traces of the service, and Microsoft's sign-in logs, which can include IP addresses and e-mail addresses): at most 30 days.
  • FS Manager transfer session records: deleted after 30 days without activity.
  • Mailgun's delivery log of each message (recipient address, time and delivery status): up to 30 days. Depending on the service plan, Mailgun may also keep a copy of the message for at most 7 days.
  • Your user account: for as long as you have access to the service. It is removed when you no longer need access, or when you ask for it.

5Cookies and browser storage

The service uses one cookie, the sign-in session cookie (AppServiceAuthSession), which is strictly necessary for signing in. Microsoft's sign-in page sets its own cookies under Microsoft's privacy statement. Your browser's local storage keeps the competition you have selected and a few display preferences, on your own device.

The service does not use cookies or anything else for analytics, marketing or tracking, and no visitor data is collected. The fonts are served from this site, so loading a page does not contact any third party.

6Your rights

You have the right to access the data concerning you, to have inaccurate data corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing and to object to it. If a photo of you has been used on the basis of consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time. A parent or guardian can make a request on behalf of a minor.

Send your request to markus@lintuala.fi. We reply within one month.

If your name has already been published on a results site, ask that site to remove it. With the GDPR Removal Tool its operator replaces your name and club with the text “Nimi poistettu pyynnöstä” (name removed on request), while placements and scores stay in place so that the other skaters' results remain correct.

7Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe your data has been processed in breach of data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi). We hope you will contact us first so that the matter can be put right quickly.
