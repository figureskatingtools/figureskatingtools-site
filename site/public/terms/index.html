2The service

The service is a set of tools for figure skating result services and competition organisers. It includes tools that produce a competition's documents from FS Manager data, tools that run entirely in your browser, tools that process data in memory without storing it, and a live link that receives competition data from FS Manager. It produces a competition's paperwork; it does not publish results.

These terms cover every tool of the service, including tools released after you accepted them, from the day they are released. When a tool processes a new kind of personal data, we update the privacy notice.

Figure Skating Manager (FS Manager) is a third-party product. We are not affiliated with its maker, and the live link depends on FS Manager working as documented.

Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose

We store competition data and produce documents from it with the tools (such as judges' papers, the protocol, readable score sheets and redacted versions of a result service's own published result files), send judges' papers by e-mail, make files available for download and let users share a competition with other users. The purpose is to produce the paperwork for the competition at hand. Processing lasts until the competition's data is deleted.

Data subjects and types of data

  • Skaters and team members: name, club, nation, category, starting order, placement, scores and withdrawal. FS Manager's participant files also carry gender, nationality, the events and team a participant is registered for and FS Manager's participant codes. Dates of birth are removed from them before they are stored.
  • People in podium and team photos: their photo. They may be minors.
  • Judges and other competition officials: name and role.
  • Recipients of the judges' papers e-mail, including the result service's own address that receives a copy of every message: e-mail address, and the send log.
  • People whose name a result service removes from its own published results: name and club as they appear in those results.

The service is not meant for special categories of personal data, such as health data. Do not upload them.
