The service is a set of tools for figure skating result services: Judge Paper Creator, Protocol Generator, Score Modifier, the Competition Banner Generator, the GDPR Removal Tool and the live link that receives competition data from FS Manager. It produces a competition's paperwork; it does not publish results.

  • Only upload or send the data the competition needs, and only data you are entitled to provide, for example data you have received from the organiser or from FS Manager for that competition.
  • Use a podium or team photo only when the people in it have a valid photography consent, for a minor given by a parent or guardian (see the privacy notice, section 2).
  • Keep the data accurate, and correct or delete it when it is not.
  • Do not upload anything unlawful, anything that infringes someone else's rights, or anything harmful such as malware.

  • Share a competition only with people who need it for the competition, and remove their access when they no longer do.
  • Everyone with access to a competition sees its data and each other's names and e-mail addresses, as described in the privacy notice, section 3.
  • Users a competition is shared with are bound by these terms in the same way as its owner.

7Data processing terms

This section is the agreement on processing personal data required by Article 28 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It applies to every user.

Roles

We (Markus Lintuala) are the controller of all personal data in the service, including the competition data, as stated in the privacy notice, section 1. When you use the service, you process that data on our behalf as a processor. The companies that host the service and send its e-mail (Microsoft and Mailgun) are our own processors, described in the privacy notice, section 3.

Subject matter, nature and purpose

You process competition data when you upload it, send it from FS Manager, enter, view or correct it, produce documents from it with the tools (judges' papers, the protocol, readable score sheets and redacted versions of your own published result files), send judges' papers, download files and share a competition with other users. You do so only to produce the paperwork for the competition at hand.

Duration

Processing lasts as long as you have access to the competition and its data is kept. Each competition has one deletion date, 60 days after it is created; it can be moved one week later at a time when there is a reason to, and an administrator can set another date. A competition can also be deleted earlier, and its data is then removed from every tool the following night. The full retention rules are in the privacy notice, section 4. What you must do with your own copies is under “End of processing” below.

Data subjects and types of data

  • Skaters and team members: name, club, nation, category, starting order, placement, scores and withdrawal. FS Manager's participant files may also contain other details, such as date of birth, gender and nationality.
  • People in podium and team photos: their photo. They may be minors.
  • Judges and other competition officials: name and role.
  • Recipients of the judges' papers e-mail: e-mail address, and the send log.
  • People whose name an operator removes from its own published results with the GDPR Removal Tool: name and club as they appear in those results.
  • Other users of the service: the names and e-mail addresses you see when you share a competition or work on it with others.

The service is not meant for special categories of personal data, such as health data. Do not upload them.
