1Who provides the service

figureskatingtools.com is provided and maintained by Markus Lintuala (“we”, “us”). Contact: markus@lintuala.fi.

2The service and who may use it

The service is a set of tools for figure skating result services: Judge Paper Creator, Protocol Generator, Score Modifier, the Competition Banner Generator, the GDPR Removal Tool and the live link that receives competition data from FS Manager. It produces a competition's paperwork; it does not publish results.

  • The service is meant for result service operators and the staff of competition organisers. Access is by invitation: we decide who may use it.
  • Your account is personal. Do not share your sign-in details or let anyone else use your account. Tell us at once if you suspect that someone else has used it.
  • If you use the service for a club, result service or other organisation, you confirm that you may do so on its behalf. These terms then apply to the organisation too.
  • The data you bring to the service stays yours. You allow us to process it only as far as running the service requires and as these terms describe.

3Your responsibilities

  • Only upload or send data that you may lawfully use for the competition: you need a legal basis for processing the personal data of skaters, judges and other officials, and you are responsible for informing them as data protection law requires.
  • Use a podium or team photo only when the people in it have a valid photography consent, for a minor given by a parent or guardian (see the privacy notice, section 2).
  • Keep the data accurate, and correct or delete it when it is not.
  • Do not upload anything unlawful, anything that infringes someone else's rights, or anything harmful such as malware.
  • Do not try to get around the sign-in or access controls, reach data you have no access to, or disrupt the service.
  • Check what the tools produce before you print, send or publish it. The tools work from the data they are given, and a mistake in the data or in the tools can end up in the documents.
  • Share judges' papers download links only with the people who need them: anyone who has a link can download the papers until it expires.

4Sharing competitions

The owner of a competition, normally the user who created it, can share it with other users and decide whether each of them may only view it or also edit it. Only the owner and the administrators can change who has access. The owner can take access away, and a user can leave a competition shared with them at any time.

  • Share a competition only with people who need it for the competition, and remove their access when they no longer do.
  • Everyone with access to a competition sees its data and each other's names and e-mail addresses, as described in the privacy notice, section 3.
  • Users a competition is shared with follow these terms and the owner's instructions for that competition.

7Data processing terms

This section is the agreement on processing personal data required by Article 28 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Roles

For the competition data that users upload, send from FS Manager or enter in the service, the user, or the organisation the user acts for (for example the organising club or the result service), is the controller, and we process the data on its behalf as its processor. Data about the users themselves (their account, the user directory, the administrator log and the technical logs) and the records of FS Manager senders kept for security are processed by us as controller, as described in the privacy notice; this section does not cover them.

Subject matter, nature and purpose

We store the competition data and process it with the tools to produce the competition's paperwork: judges' papers, the protocol, readable score sheets, and redacted versions of the operator's own published result files. We also receive data sent live from FS Manager, deliver judges' papers by e-mail and download link, and let the owner share the competition with other users. The data is used for no other purpose. The Competition Banner Generator runs entirely in the user's browser, so we receive nothing from it.

Duration

Processing lasts as long as the competition's data is kept. Each competition has one deletion date, 60 days after it is created; the user can move it one week later at a time when there is a reason to, and an administrator can set another date, for example when the user asks for it. The user can also delete the competition earlier. Its data is then removed from every tool the following night, and at the latest after the deletion date. The GDPR Removal Tool processes pages in memory only and keeps nothing. The full retention rules are in the privacy notice, section 4.

Data subjects and types of data

  • Skaters and team members: name, club, nation, category, starting order, placement, scores and withdrawal. FS Manager's participant files may also contain other details, such as date of birth, gender and nationality.
  • People in podium and team photos: their photo. They may be minors.
  • Judges and other competition officials: name and role.
  • Recipients of the judges' papers e-mail: e-mail address, and the send log.
  • People whose name an operator removes from its own published results with the GDPR Removal Tool: name and club as they appear in those results.

The service is not meant for special categories of personal data, such as health data, and users should not upload them.

Our obligations

  • Instructions. We process the data only on the user's documented instructions: these terms and the user's use of the service's functions (such as uploading, generating, sending, sharing and deleting). If we believe an instruction breaks data protection law, we will tell the user. We process data otherwise only if the law requires it, and then tell the user unless the law forbids it.
  • Confidentiality. Apart from the competition's owner and the users it is shared with, only the service's administrators (currently us) can access competition data. They do so only when needed, for example when a user asks for help or to keep the service running, and they are bound to confidentiality.
  • Security. We protect the data with appropriate technical and organisational measures (GDPR Article 32), including mandatory sign-in, access limited to the competition's owner, the users it is shared with and the administrators, encrypted connections and storage, and logging of administrative actions.
  • Sub-processors. The user gives a general authorisation to use sub-processors. We currently use Microsoft (the Azure cloud, which hosts the service in its Sweden Central region in the EU) and Mailgun (sending the judges' papers e-mail through its EU region), as listed in the privacy notice, section 3, which also describes the safeguards for any access from the United States. We will tell users in advance before adding or replacing a sub-processor, and a user who objects can stop using the service and delete their competitions before the change. Each sub-processor is bound by data protection obligations equivalent to these.
  • Data subjects' requests. We help the user, as far as we reasonably can, to answer requests from data subjects who use their rights. If a data subject contacts us about competition data, we will pass the request on to the user or help them answer it.
  • Personal data breaches. We will tell the user without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting their competition data, and help them meet their own obligations, such as notifying the supervisory authority.
  • Other assistance. We help the user, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to us, with security and with any data protection impact assessment.
  • Deletion. When processing ends, the competition data is deleted as described under Duration, and we keep no copies of it. Only the competition's name, dates and usage counts remain, as statistics without personal data. Traces in the technical logs and in Mailgun's delivery log expire within the times stated in the privacy notice.
  • Audits. On reasonable request, we give the user the information needed to show that these obligations are met, and allow and contribute to audits, primarily by answering in writing. Ask at markus@lintuala.fi.
