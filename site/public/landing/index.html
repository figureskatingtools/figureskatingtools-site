The toolbox

Wonderful little helpers for the result service and everyone around the rink.

Judges

Judge Paper Creator

Every judge's paper set, ready and delivered.

  • Built from FS Manager PDF exports
  • A personal set for each judge, in Finnish or English
  • Sent by e-mail with a secure download link
Result service

Protocol Generator

The complete official protocol book in one go.

  • Cover, competition info, time schedule and podiums
  • Results and judges’ details straight from FS Manager
  • Team pages and rosters for synchronized skating
Beginners

Score Modifier

Score papers that skaters, parents and coaches can read.

  • Splits the “Judges Details per Skater” export
  • One clear page per skater or team
  • Easy to hand out after the event
Data protection

GDPR Removal Tool

Complete removal requests without breaking the results.

  • Fetches the published FS Manager result pages and PDFs
  • Replaces the skater’s name and club; placements and scores stay
  • Verifies every file, then hands you a ZIP. No copy is kept
Organisers

Competition Banner Generator

A branded banner for the competition in a minute.

  • Competition name, dates and venue
  • Federation and club logos
  • Ready for protocols, papers and screens
Platform

Live FS Manager link

Results arrive while the event is still running.

  • FS Manager pushes schedules, entries and PDFs directly
  • You approve each sending computer before its files are used
  • One shared competition registry behind every tool
